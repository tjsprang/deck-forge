# Deck Forge

Log your Pokémon TCG cards (search, set + number, or scan with your phone camera) and let the app build, play-test and tune the best decks from what you own.

- **Collection** — every card ever printed via the free [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev) database, with current Standard/Expanded legality and prices. Your collection is stored in your own browser (export a backup from the Collection tab).
- **Scanner** — single photos or bulk scan mode; reads the name and number, then compares artwork to find the exact printing.
- **Deck builder** — builds legal 60-card decks, play-tests thousands of openings, and tunes card counts. Decks share your collection, so the same cards can be used in every deck.
- **Tournament decks** — recent online Standard lists from [Limitless](https://play.limitlesstcg.com), what you're missing and what it costs, and real matchup results.
- **Upgrade advisor, rotation warnings, collection value & stats.**

It's a static site: open `index.html` through any web server (the camera needs https or localhost).
